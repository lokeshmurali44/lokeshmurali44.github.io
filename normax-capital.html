Product Context

01

Opening real-world assets to a broader investor audience.

Normax is a real-world asset tokenisation platform that enables investors to explore and invest in fractional ownership opportunities across art, real estate, mining, agriculture, energy, and AI and technology.

The platform connects asset discovery, KYC verification, wallet funding, USDC investment, portfolio management, yield withdrawal, buyback management and transaction tracking within one investor experience.

Learnings

What this product reinforced.

Four principles that carry into future financial and Web3 product work.

01

Familiar Patterns Reduce Web3 Complexity

Blockchain actions become easier to understand when presented within recognisable financial workflows.

02

Information Hierarchy Is Critical

Investment platforms must help users compare several financial metrics without overwhelming them.

03

State Design Is Essential

Pending, disconnected, unverified and failed states are as important as successful transactions.

04

Transparency Builds Confidence

Clear fees, balances, eligibility and transaction statuses support informed decisions.
